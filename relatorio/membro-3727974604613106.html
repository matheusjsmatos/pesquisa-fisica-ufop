                                                    3. 2021-Atual. Charge ordering effects in the differential capacitance of ionic-liquid supercapacitors
                                                      Descrição: Ionic liquid is a general designation for highly concentrated ionic solutions dissolved in a non-polar media, also referred to as molten salts. These systems have a number of interesting properties which strongly contrast with the typical one of ions in an aqueous environment. In particular, these systems are known to be stable under a variety of applied voltages, can be synthesized under different shapes and sizes, have a large conductivity and -- in spite of their large typical concentrations -- remain in a liquid state over a large window of pressures and temperatures. These key features make ionic liquids promising materials to be used as electrolytes in a new generation of high-performance capacitors, generally known as supercapacitors. From a theoretical perspective, predicting the capacitance behavior of these systems under a large range of applied voltages poses many challenging questions. The high concentrations of typical ionic liquids, together with the intrinsic long-range nature of electrostatic interactions, result in an extremely large computation cost for performing large-scale computer simulations of these systems. An efficient alternative is to rely on accurate theoretical tools such as the classical density functional theory (cDFT) and modified lattice-like approaches. These theoretical schemes can be conveniently applied withing distinct accuracy levels, allowing for a systematic investigation on how the various correlations and particle interactions influence the resulting capacitances. The aim of this project is to apply the aforementioned theoretical tools in order to access how the emergence of strong charge ordering can change both quantitative and quantitatively the differential capacitance in ionic-liquid based supercapacitors. Particular focus is given to the well-known camel-to-bell transition in the capacitance curve as a function of ionic concentration and strength couplings.. Situação: Em andamento; Natureza: Pesquisa.                                                     4. 2020-Atual. Aggregation and clustering of ultrasoft particles with competing interactions: the one-dimensional case
                                                      Descrição: Ultrasoft particles are compressible objects, usually of mesoscopic size scales, whose mutual interaction potential remains finite even in situations of full inter-particle overlap. This class of systems emerges naturally in a wide class of coarse-graining descriptions of soft-matter, whereby the hole of particles belonging to much smaller length scales is effectively incorporated as elastic-like, short range repulsion among the mesoscopic particles. Contrary to their hard-particle counterparts, ultrasoft particles can be strongly deformed upon mutual compression, leading to a much richer variety of self-assembly structures. Moreover, the ability of inter-penetration and different shape conformations in response to short-range forces result in a much wider landscape in configuration space that can be accessible to a system of ultrasoft particles. In this case, competing interactions are a key ingredient to induce close-packed particle aggregations which are absent in systems of hard particles. The aim of this project is to study the interplay between short-range particle softness and competing interactions in a system of polydisperse, ultrasoft particles. To this end, we model these particles in the context of a lattice formulation, in which each lattice site can be assigned with an arbitrary number of particles, reflecting particle inter-penetration. This model system is then described in the context of the tranfer-matrix formalism, which allows for a semi-exact treatment in one dimensional, thus allowing for a robust investigation of different assembling scenarios, efficiently spanning a wide region of parameter space.. Situação: Em andamento; Natureza: Pesquisa. Alunos envolvidos: Mestrado acadêmico: (1) . Integrantes: Thiago Escobar Colla - Coordenador / Newton Fonseca Horta - Integrante.                                                     5. 2017-Atual. Termodinâmica e Estrutura em Sistemas de Microgéis Iônicos
                                                      Descrição: Microgéis são partículas coloidais caracterizadas por uma extrema versatilidade em relação a um extenso conjunto de parâmtros externos. A possibilidade de manipular de forma simples as propriedades termodinâmicas, dinâmicas e estruturais desses sistemas frente ao controle de fatores externos ajustáveis em laboratório -- aliada a uma vasta gama de promissoras aplicações em biologia e ciência dos materiais -- vem estimulando um crescente estudo das propriedades de equilíbrio e fora-de-euilíbrio desses sitesmas nos últimos anos. O objetivo desse projeto é o de investigar as propriedades morfológicas e dinâmicas de sistemas de microgéis iônicos resultantes do balanço específico entre suas interações elásticas e eletrostáticas. Para isso, técnicas de coarse-graining são utilizadas para descrever as interações efetivas nesses sistemas, sendo posteriormente aliadas a técnicas de dinâmica molecular e método das Equações Integrais a fim de determinar propriedades estruturais sob diferentes circunstâncias. O projeto será desenvolvido em forte colaboração com grupos experimentais, visando estabelecer uma conecção promissora entre modelos teóricos e predições reais..
